What We (Don’t) Process

  • Your files: processed entirely on-device in your browser. No uploads, no server storage, no human access.
  • Operational data: basic anonymous telemetry such as page views and error events may be measured locally or via privacy-respecting analytics (if enabled). We avoid collecting personally identifiable information.
  • Ads & consent: we use a Consent Management Platform (CMP) to store your ad preferences and to communicate consent to ad vendors. Some vendors may process IP addresses and cookie/device identifiers to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices.

Cookies, Local Storage & Similar Technologies

We aim to minimize identifiers. Where used, cookies/local storage primarily power:

  • Consent storage (CMP): remembers your choices.
  • Preferences: converter options you set (e.g., output format/quality) may be saved in your browser.
  • Advertising: subject to consent; used to display and measure ads.

You can review or change your choices any time via the “Privacy” or “Cookie Settings” link in the footer.

For the current list of ad technology partners, see our CMP vendor list: /en/privacy/ (or within the CMP modal).
