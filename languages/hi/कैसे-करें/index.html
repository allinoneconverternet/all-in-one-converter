How to Convert Files in Your Browser (No Uploads)

Last updated: 19 October 2025

Follow these simple steps to convert images, audio, video, documents, and archives entirely on your device with {{BRAND}}.

Step-by-step

  1. Open a converter page: e.g., PNG, JPG, MP3, PDF.
  2. Add your file: Drag & drop or use the file picker.
  3. Choose options: Select output and quality (bitrate, sample rate, resolution, compression level).
  4. Convert: Click Convert. Processing happens locally in your browser.
  5. Save: Download the result to your device.

Quality & Size Tips

  • Images: For the web, try JPG quality 75–85 or WebP; for transparency, use PNG/WebP lossless.
  • Audio: Voice ~96–128 kbps; music ~192–256 kbps AAC/MP3; for lossless keep FLAC/ALAC.
  • Video: Lower resolution/bitrate to shrink size; H.264 compatibility is broad; VP9/AV1 may be smaller but slower to encode on some devices.
  • Documents: For scanned PDFs, enable compression/“optimize” when available.

Troubleshooting

  • Unsupported file? Try a different input or update your browser.
  • Large file stalls? Close other heavy tabs and retry with lower quality settings.
  • Offline? Some features require connectivity (ads/consent checks). Reconnect and reload.
