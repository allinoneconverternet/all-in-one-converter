Security at {{BRAND}}

Last updated: 19 October 2025

{{BRAND}} performs file conversions entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers. Below is how we protect users and the app itself.

Threat Model & Philosophy

  • Data minimization: files and sensitive content stay on your device.
  • Transport security: all pages are served over HTTPS with HSTS enabled.
  • Isolation: heavy processing uses WebAssembly/Web Workers where available to keep tasks sandboxed in the browser.

Application Security Practices

  • Content Security Policy (CSP): restrictive defaults to limit script origins and inline code.
  • Subresource Integrity (SRI): third-party scripts/styles guarded with integrity attributes where applicable.
  • Headers: COOP/COEP/CORP and Permissions-Policy used to reduce cross-origin risks and lock down powerful APIs.
  • Supply chain: dependency updates tracked and security patches applied promptly.
  • Ads sandboxing: ad slots use fixed sizes and sandboxed iframes to minimize layout shift and reduce risk.

Note: exact policies may vary by deployment; if you’re a researcher, contact us for details.

Your Files & Data

  • No uploads: conversions happen locally in your browser.
  • No retention: we don’t store your files on our servers.
  • Ads & consent only: we may process limited identifiers to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent preferences. See our Privacy Policy.

Vulnerability Disclosure

If you believe you’ve found a security issue, please email {{SECURITY_EMAIL}} with details. For responsible disclosure, we’ll acknowledge your report and work to resolve the issue quickly.

Optional: we also serve /.well-known/security.txt with our current contact information.

Service Availability

As an in-browser tool, {{BRAND}} depends on your device’s performance and browser capabilities. We aim to support current versions of major browsers on desktop and mobile.
