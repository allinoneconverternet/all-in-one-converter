Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 19 October 2025

Your quick answers about privacy, speed, supported formats, browsers, ads, and troubleshooting.

Top questions

Do you upload my files to your servers?

No. Conversions happen entirely in your web browser using local processing. Files never leave your device and are not stored on our servers.

What data do you collect?

We do not collect your files. We run an ad-supported service and process limited ad-related consent data to display and measure ads. Adjust your preferences in the cookie/consent settings.

Which formats are supported?

See the format index on the home page and the converter pages for details (e.g., PNG, JPG, MP3, PDF).

Is {{BRAND}} free to use?

Yes, supported by advertising. You control your ad preferences via the CMP modal.

Why is conversion so fast?

Processing is local—no network transfers of large files—so speed is limited mainly by your device and chosen options.

Does it work offline?

If assets are cached and your browser supports the required features, some conversions may work offline. Ads and vendor checks require connectivity.

Are there file size limits?

Limits depend on your device memory/CPU and browser constraints. For very large media, try lower bitrate/resolution or split files.

Which browsers are supported?

Current versions of major browsers on desktop and mobile. Keep your browser updated for best results.

Can I delete data you hold about me?

We don’t hold your files. For ad-related identifiers, withdraw consent in the CMP modal or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

How do I contact support?

Visit Contact or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
